Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

OmniCast ("the app") is a social video publishing tool. It lets you upload a video once and publish it to social media accounts you connect (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook Pages, Instagram business accounts) on a schedule you choose.

Data we collect

  • Account credentials — email and password you register with (password stored as a salted hash).
  • OAuth tokens — access and refresh tokens for the social accounts you explicitly connect, stored encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). We never see or store your social platform passwords.
  • Content you upload — videos, thumbnails, captions, titles, hashtags and scheduling parameters you create in the app.
  • Channel metadata — public profile/channel names, avatars and IDs of the accounts you connect, used to display your connected channels.

How we use it

  • Authenticate you and keep your session.
  • Publish your content to the platforms and accounts you select, at the time you schedule.
  • Display publishing status, errors and history back to you.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not access your social accounts for any purpose other than the publishing actions you request.

Third-party platforms

Publishing is performed through the official APIs of YouTube (Google), TikTok, and Meta (Facebook/Instagram). Your use of those platforms is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Data retention & deletion

Videos and tokens are retained until you delete them or delete your account. See the data deletion instructions for how to remove your data.
