User Data Deletion

Last updated: 18 November 2026

You can delete your OmniCast data at any time in either of these ways:

Option 1 — inside the app

  • Disconnect a social account: Accounts page → remove the connected channel. Its stored OAuth tokens are deleted immediately.
  • Delete a video: Library → delete. The video file, thumbnail and related scheduled posts are removed.
  • Delete your account: Settings → delete account. This removes your login, all connected accounts, tokens, videos and publish history.

Option 2 — email request

Email trunkynw2803@gmail.com from the address you registered with, subject "Data deletion". We delete your account and all associated data within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

Your login credentials, all stored OAuth tokens (encrypted copies are purged), uploaded videos and thumbnails, scheduled posts, publish jobs and history. Content already published to third-party platforms is not recalled — delete it from the platform itself if needed.
